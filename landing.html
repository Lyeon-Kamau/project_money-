What We Do

Our system reads your M-Pesa transaction messages and converts them into structured financial data.

Instead of manually checking SMS messages, you get a full dashboard showing: Total Sent, Total Received, Net Balance, and Full Transaction History.

How It Works

1. Login securely using your phone number (OTP authentication).

2. Import SMS messages or upload transaction files.

3. Our system automatically detects sent & received transactions.

4. View analytics and export reports anytime.

Why It Matters

Financial awareness leads to better financial decisions. By centralizing your M-Pesa data, you gain clarity, control, and smarter tracking of your money.
